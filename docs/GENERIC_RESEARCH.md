# Generic business and location research

The November 2 user direction expands Tickr beyond four business types. The legacy `vertical` field now stores a bounded free-form category. A missing category does not stop an attributable business match; specialized historical playbooks are optional guidance over a shared generic workflow.

## Research path

1. Resolve the supplied reference. A Maps routing redirect can provide the business name, actual pin coordinates, viewport coordinates and place/feature identifiers. These are hints, not a fetched Maps listing or proof of address.
2. Use Meta-native retrieval to identify the business and investigate its exact location. Preserve the difference between a matched branch, a source-linked candidate and an unresolved branch. Do not choose a convenient branch from a chain profile.
3. Run an adaptive investigation using the business category, goal, actual current date, known sources and remaining gaps. Research offerings/services, brand story, customer signals, nearby comparisons, seasonal context and useful content/operating details. Record the research plan, queries, returned URLs and coverage limitations.
4. Collect permitted original official-site text and analyze the combined source ledger. Keep customer and competitor signals separate from the business's own capabilities.
5. Select an evidenced opportunity and generate a source-bound brief. The brief connects findings to campaign timing, a real offering or story, and four Reel scenes. A seasonal idea such as Diwali is labelled a proposed theme unless a current original source establishes a specific offer for the location.
6. Compose seven channel versions with a source image when accessible or a clearly labelled Meta illustration, then render the actual Reel and package the reviewed concept inventory.

Discovery has up to five native actions; investigation has up to ten in a separate request. A product illustration plus SAM uses six Meta model requests: four text requests, one image request and one segmentation request. A story illustration usually uses five. The reservation ceiling is six, with at most two visual requests. These limits prevent an unbounded crawl. Coverage is a report of inspected material, never a claim to have scraped the entire web.

## Source and branch boundaries

Sources retain acquisition type and exact retrieval references. Optional context records branch scope, publication/validity dates, the supporting text for those fields and temporal status. Retrieval time does not prove publication date. Invalid dates, inverted validity windows and unanchored context are rejected. Older dated article URLs may conservatively exclude a source from current campaigns without inventing a verified publication date.

A city-only reference cannot confirm a particular neighbourhood branch. Place-pin and place-ID conflicts remain unresolved; viewport coordinates are not a shop location. Another branch's menu, expired offer or historical launch cannot support a current product promise at the requested location. Candidate branch names and addresses remain reported possibilities, never silently copied into a confirmed locality.

AI-linked summaries remain reported claims. An actually opened URL establishes attribution, not original wording or independent verification. Current promotional status requires stronger original, dated, location-specific evidence. Proposed festivals, offers and CTAs remain distinct from established business operations.

## Campaign durability and provenance

A completed copy response is saved privately with input/content/request hashes and actual metering before semantic validation. If validation fails, an explicit retry can revalidate those exact saved bytes after a fix without a new copy request. It cannot accept invalid content or replace missing/corrupted responses automatically. Completed research, copy and image checkpoints remain reusable under their binding rules.

The exported dossier retains the research insights, branch scope, creative rationale, proposed seasonal theme, source map and image provenance. It accompanies actual PNGs, a four-scene MP4, captions, suggested conversation scripts and checksummed ZIP. The output remains `concept_needs_review`; no social publication, messaging automation, ad spend or measured conversion uplift is implied.

All runtime inference remains Meta-hosted. Public official-site/static-image HTTP access uses the separately bounded, credential-free reader. No third-party search, Google Places or additional inference service was added.
