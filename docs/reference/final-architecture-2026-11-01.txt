## 1. The product we're building

An autonomous growth studio for businesses that are better in real life than they appear online.

A bakery, dentist, gym, or salon enters its website or business listing. Our AI investigates its actual strengths, customers, local competitors, and market opportunities, then builds a coordinated marketing campaign tailored to Facebook, Instagram, Reels, and WhatsApp.

The difference from conventional AI marketing software is that the business doesn't need to know what to advertise, which audience to target, or how to write a creative brief.

The intelligence engine makes those decisions and shows its evidence.

Our product has three non-negotiable principles:

1. Intelligence before generation. Research and diagnose the business before creating anything.
2. One strategy, multiple native executions. A Reel, Instagram post, Facebook ad, and WhatsApp-directed ad must all advance the same campaign objective, but use different creatives, copy, and calls to action.
3. Meta-only AI and platform APIs. No OpenAI, Google Places, external search service, video-generation provider, or third-party marketing intelligence API.

Local rendering, storage, browser processing, and open-source software libraries are allowed. They are not external AI services.

## 4. The most important subsystem: the intelligence engine

This is where most of our engineering effort should go.

### A. Collect evidence, never invent it

Every extracted claim becomes a structured evidence object.

```

{
  "claim_id": "ev_001",
  "business_id": "bakery_01",
  "claim": "The bakery offers sourdough bread",
  "source_type": "official_website",
  "source_url": "https://example.com/menu",
  "confidence": 0.96,
  "verification": "source_supported",
  "advertising_allowed": true
}

```

The URL above is illustrative. In execution, the evidence object stores the real source, retrieval time and supporting excerpt.

The key isn't just storing facts. We must distinguish:

- Verified fact: A treatment, price, service, location, product or opening hour supported by a source.
- Observed signal: A recurring theme in genuine reviews or a competitor's public advertising.
- Inference: A plausible business opportunity or audience hypothesis.
- Proposal: A new offer, discount or campaign idea requiring owner approval.

A campaign can recommend a new ₹299 combination offer. It cannot announce that offer as already available without approval.

### B. A single URL is an entry point, not guaranteed complete data

To satisfy the Meta-only restriction:

| Input source          | Collection method                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Business website      | Direct HTTP/HTML retrieval, with domain restrictions and safe crawling                   |
| Google listing URL    | Business identifier + Meta-grounded research; optional user-supplied listing screenshots |
| Competitors           | Meta Model API web-search grounding                                                      |
| Public reviews        | Only content legitimately accessible through the permitted research methods              |
| Instagram performance | Authorized Meta Instagram API, when connected                                            |
| Ad performance        | Authorized Meta Marketing API, when connected                                            |
| Business photographs  | Owner upload or authorized media                                                         |
| Offer details         | Extracted evidence or one targeted owner confirmation                                    |

Directly reading a business's public website isn't calling a third-party API. But we will not scrape Google Maps behind access restrictions or pretend we have full Google review coverage.

Meta's Model API includes built-in web search with source citations. Importantly, the model decides whether a search is performed, and coverage is not exhaustive. We'll inspect actual search calls and citations rather than assuming a research request succeeded.&#x20;

### C. The Digital Opportunity Gap

I would make this the central intelligence feature.

We calculate three independent dimensions rather than manufacturing a single mysterious score.

Reputation

Review quality, trust signals, strengths

Visibility

Discoverability, content, clarity

Conversion

Offers, CTAs, booking friction

Evidence-backed opportunities

The engine converts gaps and verified differentiators into ranked marketing hypotheses.

Our internal ranking model:

\\[ O = 0.30I + 0.25E + 0.20D + 0.15F + 0.10C \\]

Where each input is scored on a consistent 0–100 scale:

- \\(I\\): estimated business impact
- \\(E\\): strength of supporting evidence
- \\(D\\): competitive differentiation
- \\(F\\): feasibility with current business assets
- \\(C\\): confidence in the diagnosis

These are proposed engineering weights, not scientifically calibrated business performance predictions. We'll display data coverage separately so poor evidence cannot masquerade as certainty.

The output must explain the top three opportunities and why the selected one is superior.

### D. Strategy before creative

Every selected opportunity produces a structured campaign blueprint:

```

{
  "campaign": "The 4 PM Ritual",
  "vertical": "cafe_bakery",
  "objective": "increase_afternoon_visits",
  "audience": "local afternoon coffee customers",
  "differentiator": "verified_signature_pastry",
  "core_message": "Make the afternoon worth a visit",
  "offer_status": "no_discount_required",
  "primary_cta": "Visit us this afternoon",
  "alternate_cta": "Ask us on WhatsApp",
  "channels": [
    "facebook_ads",
    "instagram_feed",
    "instagram_reels",
    "click_to_whatsapp"
  ],
  "approved_claim_ids": ["ev_001"],
  "requires_owner_approval": true
}

```

Each platform renderer receives the same blueprint and transforms it into a channel-appropriate execution.

This prevents the four channels from drifting into unrelated content.

### WhatsApp needs a conversion experience, not just an ad

For Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns, our kit should generate:

- An ad creative and primary copy.
- A conversation-opening prompt matched to the campaign.
- Three suggested business replies for common enquiries.
- A lead qualification question.
- A suggested conversion action, such as making a reservation or booking a consultation.

For example, the bakery's conversation prompt could be: "Hi, I'd like to know what's fresh this afternoon."

For the dentist, it might be: "Hi, I'd like to ask about booking a consultation."

This creates a more credible connection between creative strategy and business outcome.

WhatsApp Cloud API templates are a separate messaging mechanism that can require approval; we won't confuse them with ad creatives or silently initiate marketing messages.&#x20;

## 6. The four vertical intelligence packs

All four businesses share the same engine, but each uses a distinct diagnostic framework.

| Vertical      | Intelligence focus                                         | Campaign objective              | Special QA                                                 |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Café / Bakery | Product desirability, locality, occasions, menu visibility | More in-store visits            | Real product fidelity, valid prices                        |
| Dentist       | Credentials, reassurance, education, convenience           | Qualified consultations         | Healthcare claims, privacy, professional advertising rules |
| Gym           | Coaching, facilities, membership value, community          | Trial enquiries and memberships | No unrealistic body transformations                        |
| Salon         | Portfolio, services, trends, stylist differentiation       | Appointments and repeat visits  | Consent, genuine work, accurate results                    |

Our demo will deliberately show different strategic decisions. A dentist should not sound like a bakery with the nouns changed.

We will maintain a small vertical rules file for each category, while keeping research, planning, creative generation, quality control and exporting completely shared.

### Three engineering rules

1\. Every API call is centrally routed. The application uses one Meta API client with rate limiting, retries, timeouts and usage logging. A model must never receive unrestricted network access.

2\. Every major stage produces a validated artifact. Research outputs a `BusinessProfile`; diagnosis outputs `OpportunityReport`; planning outputs `CampaignBlueprint`; generation outputs `CreativeManifest`; evaluation outputs `QualityReport`.

Meta supports schema-constrained structured outputs, so we can use strict JSON schemas rather than brittle parsing of free-form responses.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://dev.meta.ai\&sz=32)

Meta Model API



3\. We checkpoint after each stage. If image generation fails, the already completed research and campaign strategy must not have to run again.

### Security and reliability boundaries

We'll allow only approved domain retrieval, block private/local network destinations to prevent SSRF, restrict file sizes, store Meta credentials server-side, and sanitize all content used as model inputs.

Information collected from websites will be treated as untrusted evidence, never instructions for the agents.

For the demo, a run will have a fixed research budget, generation budget and revision limit. Missing evidence should produce an explicit unknown or clarification request, not a fabricated answer.

## 9. How we demonstrate winning-level technical ambition

Our central hypothesis is that an evidence-backed multi-stage intelligence engine produces more distinctive, accurate and useful campaigns than a direct instruction to generate marketing content.

We need to test that hypothesis, not merely assert it.

## Evaluation protocol

BASELINE A

Direct generation

Business URL + generic campaign instruction → Muse Spark output

SYSTEM B

Our intelligence engine

Research → evidence → opportunity ranking → strategy → creative → critique

Both approaches use Meta models and the same permitted business inputs. Outputs are scored on groundedness, differentiation, platform suitability, authenticity and actionability.

Our proposed acceptance criteria:

| Metric                          | Target                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| End-to-end campaign generation  | 4/4 benchmark businesses                                            |
| Critical fabricated claims      | 0                                                                   |
| Channel completeness            | 100%                                                                |
| Valid, playable Reel files      | 4/4                                                                 |
| Verified product/brand fidelity | Pass all owner-supplied asset checks                                |
| Creative quality                | At least 4/5 average in blind human review                          |
| Baseline comparison             | Our pipeline scores higher on groundedness and business specificity |

For a fair comparison, we must report the actual scores, even if our system doesn't outperform the baseline on every dimension.

I also want to capture three technical signals for the judges: how the agent changes its research between verticals, how evidence affects campaign selection, and whether the critic catches and fixes a bad creative.

A deliberate failure-and-recovery demonstration could be more impressive than a flawless-looking recording that hides the underlying intelligence.

## 10. What the final interface should feel like

There are only five screens:

01 · Discover

Paste the business URL, confirm location and optionally add original photos. One primary action: Analyze my business.

02 · Investigate

Live agent activity, evidence found, competitors identified and gaps detected. A transparent process, not a fake progress bar.

03 · Opportunities

Three ranked campaign opportunities with evidence, proposed audience, objective and differentiation. The agent recommends one.

04 · Creative studio

View the campaign kit in platform-native previews, watch the Reel, compare variants and revise individual assets.

05 · Launch kit

Download media, campaign copy, channel plan, recommended setup and evidence report. Publishing integrations appear only when authorized.
